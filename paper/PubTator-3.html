01Motivation

Keyword matching can miss relevant biomedical evidence

  • Different names for the same biomedical concept can reduce recall in keyword searches.
  • Articles can mention both query entities without evidence for the requested relationship.
EntitiesMap synonymous names to a shared biomedical identifier.
RelationsSpecify a relationship (e.g., a chemical reducing gene expression).

02Method

PubTator enables entity and relation search

  • PubTator resolves synonymous entity names and retrieves articles with evidence for specific relationships.
03Method

PubTator annotates new biomedical articles weekly

  • AIONER identifies 6 entity types; normalization maps entity mentions to database identifiers.
  • BioREx extracts 12 relation types for 8 entity-type pairs.
04Resource

PubTator provides large-scale biomedical annotations

  • PubTator searches PubMed abstracts and full-text articles in the PMC Open Access Subset.
  • The web interface enables interactive search; APIs and bulk downloads enable programmatic use.

  • GPT-4 uses 3 PubTator APIs to retrieve evidence for biomedical relationships.

07Limitations

The 2024 PubTator system extracts relations from abstracts

  • In the 2024 system, relation extraction uses abstracts only; entity annotation also includes full texts.
  • Relation extraction includes 12 types, and automated annotations can contain errors.
  • The case studies evaluate 12 entity-pair queries and 8 questions; broader retrieval and clinical performance require further evaluation.
Source: Discussion, p. W545; case studies, pp. W543–W544.
