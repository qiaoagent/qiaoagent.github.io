I am a medically trained AI researcher at [NIH](https://www.nih.gov), supported by the K99/R00 Pathway to Independence Award ($997,000, one of [about three](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/ep/FAQK99_R00Grants.html#:~:text=On%20average%2C%20NLM%20has%20funded,over%20the%20last%20few%20years.) awarded per year by [NLM](https://www.nlm.nih.gov)). I develop AI agents to generate and utilize medical evidence, with the long-term goal of building an open infrastructure that connects evolving medical evidence, real-world information needs, and trustworthy AI agents.

My research spans biomedical language modeling, information retrieval, retrieval-augmented generation, and medical AI evaluation. Studies that I led (e.g., [PubMedQA](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1259/) and [TrialGPT](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-53081-z)) or co-led have appeared in [Nature Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02748-6), [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-53081-z) [Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64430-x), [Nature Protocols](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41596-026-01408-z), [npj Digital Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-024-01185-7), [NeurIPS](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/99e81750f3fdfcaf9613db2dbf4bd623-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html), [ACL](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.372/), [EM](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1259/)N[LP](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.114/), and [SIGIR](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3539618.3592005). In total, our work has over 9,000 citations on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=tYy-bzgAAAAJ&hl=en).

We developed the [MedCPT](https://huggingface.co/ncbi/MedCPT-Query-Encoder) [foundation](https://huggingface.co/ncbi/MedCPT-Article-Encoder) [models](https://huggingface.co/ncbi/MedCPT-Cross-Encoder) that have been downloaded over 10 million times & powering [NLM products](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/research/litsense2/) that serve millions, and [PubTator Database](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/research/pubtator3/) that has been accessed over 1 billion times. Our work has also been adopted by [Google](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06291-2), [Microsoft & OpenAI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13375), [Meta](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09085), [Anthropic](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/de8ba9b01c9ab7cbabf5c33b80b7bbc618857627/Model_Card_Claude_3.pdf), [Nvidia](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/customizing-nvidia-nims-for-domain-specific-needs-with-nvidia-nemo/); and featured by [NIH](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/nih-findings-shed-light-risks-benefits-integrating-ai-into-medical-decision-making) [News](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/nih-developed-ai-algorithm-matches-potential-volunteers-clinical-trials) [Releases](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/nih-researchers-develop-ai-agent-improves-accuracy-gene-set-analysis-leveraging-expert-curated-databases) & [Catalyst](https://irp.nih.gov/catalyst/33/3/homegrown-ai), [POLITICO](https://www.politico.com/newsletters/prescription-pulse/2024/11/19/using-ai-to-match-patients-to-clinical-trials-00190301), [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-024-00753-x), [Nature Biotech](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-025-02754-1), [MedScape](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/ais-limitations-clinical-decision-making-2024a1000ffn?form=fpf).
