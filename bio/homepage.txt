I am a medically trained AI researcher at NIH, supported by the K99/R00 Pathway to Independence Award ($997,000, one of about three awarded per year by NLM). I develop AI agents to generate and utilize medical evidence, with the long-term goal of building an open infrastructure that connects evolving medical evidence, real-world information needs, and trustworthy AI agents.

My research spans biomedical language modeling, information retrieval, retrieval-augmented generation, and medical AI evaluation. Studies that I led (e.g., PubMedQA and TrialGPT) or co-led have appeared in Nature Methods, Nature Communications, Nature Protocols, npj Digital Medicine, NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, and SIGIR. In total, our work has over 9,000 citations on Google Scholar.

We developed the MedCPT foundation models that have been downloaded over 10 million times & powering NLM products that serve millions, and PubTator Database that has been accessed over 1 billion times. Our work has also been adopted by Google, Microsoft & OpenAI, Meta, Anthropic, Nvidia; and featured by NIH News Releases & Catalyst, POLITICO, Nature, Nature Biotech, MedScape.

Our research was highlighted by NIH in its budget justification sent to the United States Congress.
