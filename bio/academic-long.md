Qiao Jin, MD, is a Research Fellow at the National Library of Medicine (NLM), National Institutes of Health (NIH), supported by an NIH K99/R00 Pathway to Independence Award. Dr. Jin develops AI agents to generate and utilize medical evidence, with the long-term goal of building an open infrastructure that connects evolving medical evidence, real-world information needs, and trustworthy AI agents. He has published over 50 papers in medical AI with more than 9,000 citations. His research has appeared in Nature Methods, Nature Machine Intelligence, Nature Communications, Nature Protocols, npj Digital Medicine, NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP and SIGIR. Dr. Jin introduced TrialGPT, an AI system for matching patients to clinical trials, which NIH highlighted in its budget justification to the United States Congress. He developed the MedCPT foundation models, which have been downloaded over 10 million times and power NLM products serving millions. His benchmarks PubMedQA and MedCalc-Bench are used by Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Meta, Anthropic and Nvidia. Dr. Jin serves as an Area Chair for NeurIPS and ACL Rolling Review, an editor for JAMIA, and an associate editor for JMIR. He received his BSc and MD from Tsinghua University.
