# Sources for "Why I left medicine for AI"

- Qiao Jin, 我为什么弃医从 AI, Xiaohongshu, 2024-12-01: the original post; the narrative, the "green code" image, PubMed's search volume, the three-months-before-ChatGPT timing and the closing line come from it.
- 魏则西事件, Chinese Wikipedia: Wei Zexi born 1994-02-18, died 2016-04-12; computer science student at Xidian University (Xi'an); advanced synovial sarcoma found in 2014 in his second year; chemotherapy and radiotherapy first; Baidu's paid ranking led the family to the Beijing Armed Police Corps Second Hospital's tumour biology centre; DC-CIK "biological immunotherapy" claimed 80–90% effective and a Stanford collaboration; still at the research stage in the US, with trials not continued after 2015; more than 200,000 yuan spent with no effect.
- Wei Zexi, English Wikipedia (summary): 21-year-old student; DC-CIK; learned of the hospital from a promoted Baidu result.
- Beijing Times (京华时报), 2016-05-04, "斯坦福大学回应'魏则西事件'：未与中方医院合作", syndicated at https://health.huanqiu.com/article/9CaKrnJV8O1 and https://www.163.com/gov/article/BM7CTCG400234II1.html: Stanford School of Medicine media relations told the paper Stanford had never collaborated with a Chinese hospital and had no connection to the case; the hospital's own material claimed a 2009 Stanford collaboration.
- 200,000 yuan ≈ US$30,000 at the 2016 rate (about 6.5 yuan per dollar).
- Qiao Jin's timeline (2017–2019 Pittsburgh, 2019–2022 PUMCH rotations plus lab and Alibaba DAMO, 2022 NIH): his CV.
- The photo of Wei Zexi's parents holding his portrait: Wan Jia (万家), Caixin photographer, from the photo story "魏则西的葬礼" (Caixin, 2016-05-01, https://photos.caixin.com/2016-05-01/100938827.html; 13 frames shot in Xianyang, Shaanxi, 11–13 April 2016, with the father's permission).
